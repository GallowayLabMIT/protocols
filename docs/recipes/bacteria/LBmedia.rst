========================
LB selection plates
========================


1. Dissolve 32 g of LB agar powder in 800 mL of sterile/ELGA water.
2. Add stir bar to the bottle, leave unscrewed, and cover with aluminum foil. 
3. Autoclave on 20 min liquid cycle at 121°C.
4. Wait for the solution to cool to about 45-50°C by putting on stir plate at room temperature.

.. tip::
  If you can keep touching it with bare hands, that indicates the temperature of the LB is around 45-50°C and it's time to add antibiotics.
  It typically takes only 20-30 minutes to cool down to this temperature.

5. Add ~800 uL of 1000x antibiotics to the autoclaved LB agar-water solution. 
6. Dissolve the antibiotics by stirring with a stir bar. 
7. Before pouring, ensure no agar has solidified in the bottle (as a result of uneven cooling). If this happens, shake manually to re-melt the agar.
8. Pour 12-15 mL of the antibiotic-containing LB to petri dishes using a pipette.
9. Gently move the plates around to evenly distribute the agar.
  
.. tip::
  Use a 50 mL serological, fill to 60 mL, and pipette out 12 mL in sets of 5, or 15 mL in sets of 4.
  Alternatively, you can pour LB by hand if you experience solidification when pipetting.

10. Incubate the poured plates at RT until the LB agar solidifies (~1 hr minimum to overnight).
   
11.  After plates solidify, place plates back in the sleeve bag and store at 4°C. 

.. tip::
  If the plates are drying out in the incubator overnight, use 15 mL per plate and put them in the fridge soon after they solidify.
